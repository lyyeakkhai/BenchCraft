# BenchCraft Documentation

BenchCraft is an evaluation tool that tells technical founders which AI model to choose for their business problem. The founder describes the business and uploads documents. BenchCraft analyzes them, compares the top 3 fitting models, and recommends one.

## Decisions so far

- Revenue model: monthly subscription (Pro plan $29 per month, planning assumption).
- Cash budget: $365, ceiling $500.
- Delivery: 5 sprints, one epic per sprint, assumed two-week sprints.
- Team: 5 members. Member 1 is Team Lead, Product Owner and Scrum Master.
- Pitch: 5 minutes, presented as a start-up, with static screens to demo the problem and solution.

## Open decisions

- Technology stack (decide in Sprint 1).
- Real names for team roles (Member 1 to 5 are placeholders).
- Real model data sources for the catalog.
